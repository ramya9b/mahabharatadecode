## LinkedIn post
The Mahabharata has a lesson buried in its ending that every ambitious person should sit with.

The Pandavas won the war. And of everyone they went to war with and for, almost no one survived. Out of armies numbered in the millions, the named survivors come to about eleven.

Ask "who won?" and the answer is clean: the Pandavas.
Ask "who survived?" and you start to feel what the win actually cost — sons buried by parents, a teacher outliving all his students, a throne inherited by a grandchild not yet born when the fighting stopped.

It's a hard reframe for anyone chasing a goal at all costs: a victory that empties the room around you is a strange kind of victory. Worth measuring a win by who's left standing, not only by who holds the prize.
