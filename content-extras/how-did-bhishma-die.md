## LinkedIn Post
There's a leadership lesson buried in one of the Mahabharata's most famous deaths, and it isn't the one people expect.

Bhishma, the grandsire of the Kuru house, held a boon: he could not die until he willed it. He was, in effect, impossible to remove. And yet his story is not about clinging to power — it's about how he used that power to leave well.

When his side was losing, he told his enemies how to bring him down. When he finally fell, he spent his last weeks not fighting but teaching the incoming ruler everything he knew about duty, law, and grief. Then he chose his moment and let go.

The hardest skill in any long career isn't endurance. It's knowing when — and how — to hand things over. Bhishma is a 3,000-year-old case study in exactly that.
