## LinkedIn Post
There's a leadership lesson hidden in one of the strangest origin stories in world literature.

Draupadi, the central woman of the Mahabharata, was not born in the ordinary way. According to the Adi Parva, a wronged king performed a sacrifice to obtain a son who could kill his rival. From that fire rose two figures: a son destined to be an assassin, and a daughter no one had requested, marked by a prophecy she never chose.

She was, in the plainest terms, created to be an instrument of someone else's revenge.

What makes her one of the most compelling figures ever written is that she refused to behave like one. Born pre-labelled, assigned a purpose before she could speak, she spent her life questioning kings and sages and insisting she was a person, not a tool.

If you've ever felt defined by circumstances you didn't choose, her story is worth an afternoon.
